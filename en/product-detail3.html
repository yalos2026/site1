Thermal Indicators

LLC "Innovative Company Yalos" is the exclusive distributor in the markets of Russia, Belarus, and Kazakhstan of specialized industrial and energy indicators from the world market leader, the Japanese company "NiGK". Temperature indicators from "NiGK" have proven themselves as a reliable tool for monitoring the temperature of heated surfaces, and their long-term use over 35 years has proven their reliability and efficiency. Hundreds of high-tech companies in Japan, Europe, the USA, and China use temperature indicators as a means of reducing accidents and ensuring the safety of production personnel.


Temperature indicators occupy their niche despite the large number of various automated means of monitoring temperature overheating. The positive characteristics of thermal indicators allow them to be used as an effective addition to any automated safety systems.

Positive qualities of thermal indicators for monitoring overheating of equipment and transmission lines in the energy sector: - Overheating is guaranteed to be detected (Continuous temperature recording); - No weather conditions affect the operation of the indicator; - No maintenance or adjustment required; - No power supply required; - Direct reading from the monitored surface; - They have the size and weight of a sticker, ultra-light, can be small-sized; - Can be installed in hard-to-reach places where the monitored surfaces are located (where it is difficult to read indicators with a beam or thermal imager); - Will serve as proof of overheating, filed as evidence in inspection and repair logs; - When equipped with fluorescent additives, it will be clearly visible in flashlight light at night; - Service life 4-10 years; - Reversible thermal indicators can be used repeatedly; - Weather conditions do not affect the indicator design - frost, heat, humidity and other natural factors; - Not afraid of oils, vapors and dirt; - When choosing the appropriate size, it will be easily visible from afar (no need to look closely); - When simultaneously installing indicators with a scale or several identical indicators with different operating temperatures, it will allow you to monitor both the threshold temperature and the preliminary one, for preventive maintenance; - Small, affordable price for everyone; - Do not compete with existing automated systems - they functionally complement them
Thermal indicators do not replace measuring instruments, they functionally complement them, becoming a mass means of monitoring the threshold levels of power equipment operation. Their use will reduce accidents, increase energy savings, and improve equipment maintenance and monitoring.

Power equipment monitoring: Monitoring the temperature of electromagnetic switches, electromagnetic contactors, electromagnetic relays, knife switches, high-voltage switches, high-voltage load switches, power capacitors and magnetic amplifiers
Power transmission equipment: - Monitoring for abnormal heat generation in connectors and jumper bushings. - Monitoring the temperature of terminals for feeder lines, negative feeder lines, overhead lines, support lines, branch connectors, jumper connectors, connectors and feeders.
