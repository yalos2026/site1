Technology of Collective Sound Virtual Reality "Yalos Stream"

New breakthrough technology for forming and controlling "close" sounds "Yalos Stream" for turning a cinema into an attraction (thereby increasing its attendance). The "Yalos Stream" technology https://yalos-project.com/is designed to create a "presence" effect for viewers at the location of events happening on the screens of cinemas and concert halls (collective sound virtual reality).

In February 2017, the company received patent No. 9,584,936 "METHOD FOR CONTROLLING SOUND IN AN AUDITORIUM" in the USA for sound control technology, and on March 14, 2017, patent RU2612997 in Russia (information published in the official ROSPATENT bulletin No. 8-2017 from 11.03.2017-20.03.2017). Referring to the obtained patents, since August 2018, the company's specialists have begun developing the "Yalos Stream" sound processor, designed for cluster reproduction of "close" sounds and controlling their movement trajectories throughout the auditorium. On December 27, 2019, our startup won the all-Russian START-1 program competition announced by the Foundation for Assistance to Innovation, Contract 0057518, application No. C1-66645, Start-19-1 competition (4th queue). We were awarded a grant for R&D to create PROTOTYPE No. 2, the "Yalos Stream" virtual reality sound processor.
INTRODUCTION TO THE PROBLEM THE PROJECT SOLVES: According to research conducted by "DOLBY", all sounds in the world around us are divided into "upper", "middle" and "close" sounds. Cinema sound systems using speakers located on the walls and ceiling of cinema halls realistically reproduce only "upper" and "middle" sounds. 1) most realistically at angles close to 90 degrees, the viewer hears "upper sounds". They are emitted by ceiling sound systems or audio speakers installed high on the walls of the cinema. 2) "middle sounds" are also realistically reproduced by viewers in the cinema. However, these sounds are emitted at an angle from 15 to 45 degrees, and for these purposes, audio speakers placed on the side and end walls of cinema halls are excellent. ATTENTION: 3) "Close sounds" cannot be realistically reproduced by any sound system, as they propagate at an angle of 0 degrees (in the horizontal plane to the viewers' hearing organs) and should be reproduced next to the viewer at a distance of 1-2 meters. In cinemas, due to the presence of acoustic devices on the walls and ceiling, and not next to the viewer, it is impossible to realistically reproduce "close sounds", i.e. the effect of "presence", as in the real world, is practically impossible to achieve. Even with such modern multi-channel sound systems as Dolby Atmos or IMAX, the viewer will never have a sense of complete presence at the location of the events being shown on the screen. The sound environment in the cinema will be most realistic only if it resembles the human surrounding world. Viewers in cinemas will be able to fully feel themselves as participants in the events happening on the screen only with the presence of "close sounds", that is, sounds that occur in life next to people at a distance from 15 cm to several meters, in the horizontal plane of hearing (footsteps, rain noise, wind, etc.). "Close sounds" significantly (up to 70%) affect the sensation of the realism of events surrounding a person.
The "Yalos Stream" technology reproduces only "close sounds" "Yalos Stream" delivers "close sounds" to the viewer in the horizontal plane, through sound reproducing devices (speakers) built into the cinema seats. The main idea of the project is to turn any cinema into a cinema attraction, with the best effect of "presence" of the viewer in the events shown on the screen (as in the surrounding world), thereby increasing cinema attendance and increasing the cost of viewing. Another significant advantage of the "Yalos Stream" technology is that this technology allows the viewer to experience collective emotions, i.e. viewers in the hall receive exciting impressions not only from the new type of sound reality, but also from the emotions of their neighbors. This effect is comparable to the effect of participants in the "roller coaster" attraction. The "presence" effect created by the new technology can be used not only in cinema, but also during various shows, concerts and attractions in amusement parks. The "YALOS STREAM" system does not compete with any existing sound system, it successfully complements them, creating a sense of collective immersion in the events happening on the screen.
Strategic goal of the project – creation of a mass, accessible and inexpensive sound system of collective virtual reality, providing viewers with a "presence" effect when watching films, concerts and shows as an additional option to existing sound equipment. Target customers - the global film industry, concert organizations, amusement parks, home electronics manufacturers, automotive industry. Target niche of the project – halls for collective viewing of films and concerts, household (home) use, cinema attractions in amusement parks. Nearest goal of the project – completion of the stage of creating prototype No. 2 - Mini cinema (prototype No. 1 has been successfully created, which confirmed the patented characteristics of the "Yalos Stream" technology). Project prospects - creation of mobile prototype No. 3 for its demonstration at world exhibitions of innovations and new technologies (for example, the NAB exhibition in Las Vegas, the AMF exhibition in Santa Monica, etc.) to find a strategic partner for joint work on implementing the technology in the global film industry. As an additional option, creating based on the mobile version of prototype No. 3, a commercial version of the Mini cinema using the "Yalos Stream" collective virtual reality technology, for use in the railway station network, in airports, in small settlements, as a school cinema for the education system.
Main advantages of Yalos Stream - The technology does not use the film's sound track - Surrounds the viewer with "close" sounds, creates the effect of the viewer's presence at the location of events on the cinema screen - Causes collective emotions of viewers when watching films together surrounded by "close" sounds - Reproduces only horizontal "close sounds" next to the viewer - Does not use channel sound recording - Uses only speakers placed in the seats of the auditorium - Can control the movement of sounds in any direction in the hall - Can reproduce sounds in any number of speakers - Reproduces sounds in the form of sound clusters of any size - Allows you to create different sound scenarios for the same film - Allows you to create sound scenarios for old films - Low cost up to $35,000 per cinema with 300-500 seats - Has options for installing speakers without replacing seats in the auditorium - Has different configurations from "Economy" to "VIP" - Technology is protected by patents in the USA and Russia - Does not compete with any sound system installed in cinemas, this is their option - Quick adaptation to any cinema, does not require sound setup.
Project stages In February 2019, prototype No. 1 was manufactured, confirming the patented characteristics of the "Yalos Stream" technology for forming and controlling the movement of sound clusters of "close sounds". Prototype No. 1 includes a sound processor, a sound matrix and software. At the beginning of work on prototype No. 2 (mini cinema), analogues of sound speakers that can be installed in cinema seats were tested. The test showed excellent results. In the video clip, you can clearly see the movement of several sound clusters of different sizes moving along given trajectories. In December 2020, a working model of a sound matrix simulating a cinema and a new version of the "Yalos Stream" 2.0 sound processor were created.
