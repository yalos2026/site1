Automated System for Monitoring Thermal Parameters of Equipment "DISKON"

Digitalization and real-time operational control is a strategic direction for the development of production process management and accident prevention. The wide development of automated systems is constrained only by the cost of their implementation. Existing forms of thermal parameter control in shielded spaces (cabinets, cells, panels, racks, etc.) offer different implementation costs. The automated hardware-software complex (PAK) "DISKON" is patented and offers an economical and effective way to monitor temperature in shielded spaces and will allow the widespread implementation of automated control means to reduce the accident rate of electrical networks and equipment.

PAK "DISKON" was developed as part of a startup that: - Won on 16.11.2021 in the final of the "Big Exploration" competition, received a grant for the development of complex elements. - Won on 28.10.2021 in the Final of the "ASTART" competition of the Novosibirsk Akademgorodok - Won and received a prize on 15.08.21 in the final of the "Technological Breakthrough" competition Orenburg - Chrysolite Valley - Won in 2022 in the final of the START-1 competition of the Foundation for Assistance to Innovation and received a grant for project development
The purposes of using DISKON are: 1. Improving the convenience, speed and efficiency of monitoring critical temperature levels of the most important units of thermal equipment, allowing maintenance personnel to immediately respond to excessive heating of control points; 2. Increasing the fire safety level of thermal equipment; 3. Reducing equipment overheating accidents through their early detection; 4. Improving operational and economic performance of equipment; 5. Reducing the time for detecting thermal equipment overheating; 6. Using an economical and reliable method of temperature monitoring; 7. Saving consumables by doing preventive, not emergency repairs; 8. Increasing personnel safety by reducing accident rates

Temperature information transmitted around the clock to the PC and smartphones of maintenance personnel. PAK "DISKON" in a 24/7 mode, in real time, transmits temperature data to the PC and smartphones of maintenance personnel. Temperature control is carried out in the range from -40C to +150C. In the software, you can set any temperature in this range as low (negative), pre-emergency and emergency. When a low temperature is detected, the sensor on the PC will turn blue, pre-emergency temperature yellow, emergency temperature red. Each sensor indicates its location for quick determination of the installation location. Information about sensors and their temperature is synchronously displayed on staff smartphones. On the smartphone, in case of detection of critical temperatures, an audible signal is also given. The software in PAK "DISKON" allows you to obtain various reports on the temperature regimes of the controlled equipment. You can find out more about the operation of PAK "DISKON" by contacting us.
