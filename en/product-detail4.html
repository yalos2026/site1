Insulation Tape

The reversible reversible thermosensitive indicator tape allows you to preserve the insulation of various cable connections. At temperatures above +70 °C, due to heating of contact connections or poor contact areas, the color of the tape changes to transparent in the heating areas. This makes it possible to quickly and easily determine the area for repair and maintenance.


The tape can be used as a replacement for thermal paints and varnishes at joints and control points.

Features - While preserving insulation integrity, it simultaneously indicates overheating temperature. - When heated above +70 °C, the tape fades and becomes transparent. Upon cooling, the tape color returns to its original state. - Standard colors: Red, Yellow, Light Blue, Green.
Additional information: The indicator tape is supplied in rolls of specified sizes with a protective strip, packaged in a paper box. It is recommended to store in the original packaging, protected from moisture, dust, heat and sunlight.
