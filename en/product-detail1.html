Overheating Indicator Marker

LLC "Innovative Company Yalos" is the exclusive supplier of thermomarkers from the Japanese company Matsui International Co., Inc. to the Russian market. The thermomarker is used to indicate overheating of buses, terminals, busbars, various components of control cabinets, transformers and other power equipment. It visually signals overheating, facilitates control and reduces equipment accidents. It can be used for screw heads and copper surfaces. The thermomarker is an irreversible type indicator, which means that after changing its color when reaching the control temperature, its color does not return to its original state, which allows you to monitor overheating around the clock. Therefore, during inspection, even after disconnecting the power supply, you can easily detect those parts and components where the temperature exceeded +65°C.

ATTENTION: Color temperature indicator in the form of a marker replaces more than 300 pcs. of conventional film thermometers Intended for indoor use only. The indicator strip drawn by the marker will begin to change color at +65°C or slightly above and will become completely red at +80°C or slightly above.
Please use the Thermomarker only for application to control objects. • Not suitable for PVC products, chloroethylene items, rubber, packaging tapes and Teflon products. • The ink contains flammable products, so please do not use the marker near fire sources. Do not burn when disposing. Please use the marker in a ventilated room. • Since the ink reacts poorly to light, please do not expose objects marked with the marker to direct sunlight (this product is designed for use in enclosed spaces and in electrical cabinets). • Please note that marks left by this product have weaker adhesion than normal ink, i.e. the ink may be scratched off if scratched with hard objects. • The shelf life of the product is one year from the date of manufacture. • Do not leave the marker in places exposed to direct sunlight and high temperatures.
