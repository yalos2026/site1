Liquid Crystal Thermometers

LLC "Innovative Company Yalos" produces liquid crystal thermometers of several types, for industrial use and for use in outerwear designed for the winter period. Temperature measurement by LCD thermometers is based on the color change of liquid crystals, they are located in the cavities of the thermometer and change color under the influence of temperature. The achievement of a certain temperature level is displayed as a color change of the corresponding numbers.


A liquid crystal thermometer in the form of a plastic strip is a type of thermometer that contains thermochromic liquid crystals that change color to indicate different temperatures. The current temperature readings are located in a green rectangle, if the green color is absent on the thermometer, then the current temperature is the average between the values on the blue and brown rectangles. On thermometers without digital values, the initial black color, when the temperature rises, the color changes to yellow, green, blue and becomes black again when the temperature is above the measured range. They are reversible and can be used again and again. The green color shows the actual temperature.

Thermometer specifications: they work due to the thermochromic effect of liquid crystals, the shell material is PET, contains no mercury, completely safe, thermometers can be washed, they are waterproof, not afraid of direct sunlight, but it is desirable to use them in a dark environment, the readings of the thermometers are clearly visible in normal lighting, so you need to illuminate the thermometer if you are surrounded by dim light. They withstand heating temperatures up to +150C for one minute, do not glow in the dark, equipped with a special edge for sewing to clothes or for inserting into a special pocket, compact and comfortable to use, unlike glass similar thermometers, they are not damaged by strong impacts, they do not need to be adjusted or serviced, all care consists of periodic cleaning of the outer surface from contamination. This procedure is performed as needed, for its implementation you do not need to use abrasive materials and aggressive chemicals, they can be washed, but you cannot keep them in water constantly, this will discolor the liquid crystals.
